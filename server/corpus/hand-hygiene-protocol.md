# Hand Hygiene and Contact Precautions Protocol

This clearly-labelled reference summary sets out the local hospital protocol for
hand hygiene and contact precautions. It applies to all clinical and support staff
entering patient care areas and is a Trust Tier 1 hospital protocol.

## Five Moments for Hand Hygiene

Perform hand hygiene at the WHO Five Moments: before touching a patient; before a
clean or aseptic procedure; after body fluid exposure risk; after touching a
patient; and after touching patient surroundings. Alcohol-based hand rub is the
default for visibly clean hands. Wash with soap and water when hands are visibly
soiled, after using the toilet, and when caring for patients with suspected or
confirmed Clostridioides difficile or norovirus, because alcohol does not reliably
kill spores.

## Contact Precautions

For patients on contact precautions, don a gown and gloves before entering the room
and remove them before leaving, performing hand hygiene immediately afterwards.
Dedicate equipment to the patient where possible, and disinfect shared equipment
with the approved sporicidal or antiviral wipe between patients. Isolate in a single
room with a dedicated bathroom when available, and display the correct precautions
signage at the entrance.

## Compliance and Audit

Hand hygiene compliance is audited monthly by ward. Report and escalate compliance
below the local target to the infection prevention and control team. Discontinue
enteric isolation only after the patient has had 48 hours of formed stool and in
line with infection prevention and control advice.
